# CAREEM Global Services Limited

## Kuhusu CAREEM · About CAREEM

**Kiswahili**

CAREEM Global Services Limited ni kampuni yenye ofisi Tanzania. Inaunganisha watafuta kazi na fursa za ajira UAE na kupanga safari za Dubai na Abu Dhabi.

**English**

CAREEM Global Services Limited has offices in Tanzania. It connects job seekers with UAE employment opportunities and plans trips to Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## Wateja wetu · Who we serve

**Kiswahili**

Tunawahudumia watafuta kazi kutoka Tanzania na Afrika Mashariki, waajiri wa UAE, na watu wanaosafiri kwa mapumziko, biashara au manunuzi.

**English**

We serve job seekers from Tanzania and East Africa, UAE employers, and people travelling for holidays, business or shopping.

## Huduma · Services overview

**Kiswahili**

Unaweza kusajili nia ya kufanya kazi, kuomba wafanyakazi, kupanga ziara au kuomba msaada kuhusu visa ya ziara, tiketi za ndege, hoteli na usafiri kati ya hoteli na uwanja wa ndege.

**English**

Register interest in work, enquire about hiring, plan tours, or request visit-visa guidance, flights, hotels and airport transfers.

## Ajira UAE · UAE jobs

**Kiswahili**

Sekta ni hoteli na ukarimu, ulinzi, usafi, udereva na usafirishaji, ujenzi na ufundi, maduka na mauzo, maghala na usafirishaji wa bidhaa, pamoja na migahawa na jikoni. Fomu pia ina Ofisi / Utawala na Nyingine. Hizi ni aina za kazi unazoweza kuchagua. Nafasi hutegemea mahitaji ya waajiri.

1. Jisajili na ueleze kazi unayotafuta.
2. Timu hupitia sifa zako, hukusaidia kujiandaa kwa usaili na kueleza mazingira ya kazi.
3. Mwajiri akikuchagua, soma na uhakiki barua ya ajira yenye mshahara, makazi na masharti kabla ya kukubali.
4. Mwajiri hushughulikia kibali cha kazi na visa kupitia mamlaka za UAE. CAREEM hukusaidia kuandaa nyaraka.
5. Kabla ya safari, utapata maelezo kuhusu sheria, utamaduni, haki zako na mawasiliano ya dharura.
6. Timu huendelea kuwasiliana nawe baada ya kufika.

Andaa pasipoti halali yenye angalau miezi sita kabla ya kuisha, CV ya sasa, vyeti vya elimu na uzoefu wa kazi, na picha za pasipoti zenye mandharinyuma meupe. Mwajiri anaweza kuomba nyaraka nyingine. Weka nakala zako mpaka wakala akuelekeze njia salama ya kuziwasilisha.

Waajiri waeleze nafasi, idadi ya wafanyakazi, mshahara, makazi na muda wanaohitaji wafanyakazi. CAREEM hutafuta na kuandaa waombaji wanaozungumza Kiswahili na Kiingereza, hupitia CV, huratibu usaili wa awali, mtandaoni au ana kwa ana, na husaidia kuratibu nyaraka na safari.

**English**

Sectors include hotels and hospitality, security, cleaning and facilities, drivers and delivery, construction and technical trades, retail and sales, warehousing and logistics, and restaurants and kitchens. The form also lists Office / Admin and Other. These are preferences. Vacancies depend on employer needs.

1. Register and specify the work you want.
2. The team reviews your profile, prepares you for interviews and explains UAE working conditions.
3. If selected, review and verify the employer’s written offer covering salary, accommodation and terms before accepting.
4. The employer processes the work permit and visa through UAE authorities. CAREEM helps prepare documents.
5. Before travel, receive a briefing on laws, culture, your rights and emergency contacts.
6. The team follows up after arrival.

Prepare a passport valid for at least six more months, an updated CV, education and work-experience certificates, and passport photos on a white background. The employer may request other documents. Keep copies until an agent explains the secure submission method.

Employers should provide roles, headcount, salary, accommodation and hiring timeline. CAREEM sources and prepares English- and Swahili-speaking candidates, screens CVs, coordinates initial interviews online or in person, and helps coordinate documents and travel.

## Safari za UAE · UAE tours and safaris

**Kiswahili**

Chagua safari ya familia, wanandoa au fungate, marafiki, biashara na manunuzi, kampuni au kikundi, au kusafiri peke yako.

- **Desert Safari ina shughuli gani?** Ziara inaelezwa kuwa na safari ya gari la 4x4 kwenye matuta ya mchanga, kupanda ngamia, kuteleza mchangani, chakula cha jioni cha BBQ na burudani kambini.
- **Dubai City Tour inafika wapi?** Vivutio ni pamoja na Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall na Fountain, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Frame na soko la dhahabu.
- **Ziara ya Abu Dhabi inaanzia wapi?** Ni ziara ya siku nzima kutoka Dubai inayojumuisha Msikiti wa Sheikh Zayed, Corniche, Qasr Al Watan na Yas Island.
- **Dhow Cruise ni nini?** Ni safari ya jahazi yenye chakula cha jioni Dubai Marina au Dubai Creek.
- **Mnapanga ziara za bustani zipi?** Unaweza kuulizia Global Village inapokuwa wazi kwa msimu, Miracle Garden, Aquaventure, IMG Worlds na Ferrari World.
- **Mnasaidia safari za manunuzi?** Ndiyo, unaweza kuomba kutembelea Deira, Dragon Mart, masoko ya jumla au maonyesho ya biashara.

Mfano wa siku tano, unaoweza kubadilishwa kulingana na bajeti, tarehe na idadi ya wasafiri:

1. Kupokelewa uwanjani, kuingia hotelini na kula chakula cha jioni kwenye jahazi.
2. Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Marina na Palm Jumeirah.
3. Kupumzika asubuhi, kisha safari ya jangwani kuanzia mchana hadi usiku.
4. Msikiti wa Sheikh Zayed na vivutio vya Abu Dhabi.
5. Manunuzi Deira au Dragon Mart, kisha kupelekwa uwanjani.

Viwanja vya kuondokea: Dar es Salaam (DAR), Zanzibar (ZNZ), Kilimanjaro (JRO), Mwanza (MWZ), Nairobi (NBO), Entebbe (EBB) na Kigali (KGL). Pia kuna chaguo la “Nyingine”. Unaweza kuomba msaada wa visa, ndege, hoteli, usafiri wa uwanjani, ziara, bima na mwongozaji wa Kiswahili. Bajeti kwenye fomu ni kwa mtu bila tiketi. Bei, hoteli na upatikanaji huthibitishwa kwa maandishi kabla ya malipo.

**English**

Choose family, couples or honeymoon, friends, business and shopping, corporate or group, or solo travel.

- **What does the Desert Safari feature?** The tour description lists a 4x4 dune drive, camel ride, sandboarding, BBQ dinner and camp entertainment.
- **Which sights are listed for the Dubai City Tour?** Sights include Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and Fountain, Dubai Marina, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Frame and the Gold Souk.
- **Where does the Abu Dhabi tour start?** It is a full-day trip from Dubai featuring Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Corniche, Qasr Al Watan and Yas Island.
- **What is a dhow cruise?** It is a dinner cruise aboard a traditional dhow at Dubai Marina or Dubai Creek.
- **Which attractions can I ask about?** You can ask about seasonal Global Village, Miracle Garden, Aquaventure, IMG Worlds and Ferrari World.
- **Do you arrange shopping trips?** Yes, you can request Deira, Dragon Mart, wholesale markets or trade fairs.

Sample five-day itinerary, adjustable for dates, budget and group size:

1. Airport pickup, hotel check-in and evening dhow cruise.
2. Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Marina and Palm Jumeirah.
3. Free morning, then a desert safari from afternoon to night.
4. Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and Abu Dhabi highlights.
5. Shopping at Deira or Dragon Mart, then airport drop-off.

Departure airports: Dar es Salaam (DAR), Zanzibar (ZNZ), Kilimanjaro (JRO), Mwanza (MWZ), Nairobi (NBO), Entebbe (EBB) and Kigali (KGL). There is also an Other option. Request visa assistance, flights, hotel, transfers, tours, travel insurance or a Swahili-speaking guide. The form asks for a budget per person excluding flights. Price, hotel and availability are confirmed in writing before payment.

## Visa, ndege na hoteli · Visa, flights and hotels

**Kiswahili**

Tovuti inaeleza visa za ziara za siku 30 au 60. CAREEM husaidia kuandaa orodha ya nyaraka, kujaza fomu na kuwasilisha ombi. Mamlaka za UAE pekee ndizo hutoa visa. CAREEM haitoi visa wala kuhakikisha ombi litakubaliwa. Masharti na ada zinaweza kubadilika. Unaweza kuomba ndege kwenda Dubai au Abu Dhabi, hoteli kulingana na bajeti na usafiri wa kwenda au kutoka uwanjani.

**English**

The site describes 30- or 60-day visit visas. CAREEM helps with document checklists, forms and submission. Only UAE authorities issue visas. CAREEM does not issue visas or guarantee approval. Requirements and fees may change. Request flights to Dubai or Abu Dhabi, a hotel within budget, and airport pickup or drop-off.
