# Majibu ya WhatsApp · WhatsApp quick replies

## Karibu · Greeting

**Kiswahili** · Karibu CAREEM Global Services Limited! Unatafuta kazi UAE, unapanga safari, au unahitaji msaada kuhusu visa?

**English** · Welcome to CAREEM Global Services Limited! Are you looking for work in the UAE, planning a trip or asking about a visa?

## Usajili · Job registration

**Kiswahili** · Jaza fomu https://careemglobal.com/register.html kisha bonyeza Send kwenye WhatsApp.

**English** · Complete https://careemglobal.com/register.html and press Send in WhatsApp.

## Nyaraka · Documents

**Kiswahili** · Andaa pasipoti halali yenye angalau miezi sita kabla ya kuisha, CV ya sasa, vyeti vya elimu na uzoefu wa kazi, na picha za pasipoti zenye mandharinyuma meupe. Mwajiri anaweza kuomba nyaraka nyingine.

**English** · Prepare a passport valid for at least six more months, an updated CV, education and work-experience certificates, and passport photos on a white background. The employer may request other documents.

## Faragha · Sensitive documents

**Kiswahili** · Tafadhali usitume hapa. Wakala atakuelekeza njia salama ya kuwasilisha nyaraka.

**English** · Please keep it out of chat. An agent will explain how to submit documents securely.

## Barua ya ajira · Job offer

**Kiswahili** · Soma mshahara, makazi na masharti kwenye barua ya ajira kabla ya kukubali. Usitume pesa kwa wakala usiyemjua. Dai risiti rasmi na hakiki barua ya ajira kwa mwajiri wa UAE.

**English** · Read the salary, accommodation and terms in the offer before accepting. Never send money to an unknown agent. Insist on an official receipt and verify any offer with the UAE employer.
