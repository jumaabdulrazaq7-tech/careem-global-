Hatua za kupata kazi UAE

Steps to a UAE job

01

Jisajili

Register

Tuma CV, nakala ya pasipoti na aina ya kazi unayotafuta kupitia WhatsApp au ofisini.

Send your CV, passport copy and the type of work you want via WhatsApp or at our office.

02

Usaili & Maandalizi

Screening & Preparation

Tunapitia sifa zako, tunakuandaa kwa usaili na kukueleza mazingira ya kazi UAE.

We review your profile, prepare you for interviews and explain UAE working conditions.

03

Offer Letter

Offer Letter

Ukichaguliwa na mwajiri, unapokea barua ya ajira yenye mshahara, makazi na masharti — ihakiki kabla ya kukubali.

If selected, you receive an offer stating salary, accommodation and terms — verify it before accepting.

04

Visa ya Kazi

Work Visa

Mwajiri hushughulikia kibali cha kazi na visa kupitia mamlaka za UAE; tunakusaidia kuandaa nyaraka.

The employer processes the work permit and visa through UAE authorities; we help you prepare documents.

05

Safari & Mafunzo

Travel & Orientation

Mafunzo mafupi kabla ya kuondoka: sheria, utamaduni, haki zako na mawasiliano ya dharura.

Short pre-departure briefing: laws, culture, your rights and emergency contacts.

06

Ufuatiliaji

Follow-up

Tunaendelea kuwasiliana nawe baada ya kufika ili kuhakikisha uko salama.

We keep in touch after you arrive to make sure you have settled safely.
